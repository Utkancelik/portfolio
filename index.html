// About Me

A developer who lives and breathes games.

I'm Utkan Çelik, a Game Developer based in Ankara, Türkiye. I'm currently building games at Brew Games in partnership with Voodoo — one of the world's top mobile game publishers.

Nearly all of that shipped work happened while I was still a student — part-time during term and full-time through summer internships, alongside my engineering degree. Since mid-2025 I've been working in games full-time.

I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Hacettepe University, but my true passion lies in game development. From game jams to published App Store titles, I love bringing ideas to life through engaging gameplay mechanics and polished experiences.
